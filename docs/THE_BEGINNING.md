## Claim ladder

- **Descriptive:** documents observed data without generalization.
- **Exploratory:** reports development-data relationships and uncertainty.
- **Candidate:** freezes a precise hypothesis for untouched evaluation.
- **Validated:** meets preregistered criteria on authorized protected data, with
  stated limitations; validation is not deployment authority.
- **Deployable:** requires separate operational evidence, controls, and approval.

## Founding principles

- Separate implementation, data-quality, and empirical evidence.
- Prefer falsifiable questions and simple baselines.
- Preserve denominators, exclusions, nulls, and failures.
- Add governance only in proportion to maturity and consequence.
- Keep the harness machinery smaller than the research.
